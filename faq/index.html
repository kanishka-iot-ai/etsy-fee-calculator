Etsy profit calculator FAQs

Quick answers about estimated fees, prices, and what the ShopProfit calculator includes.

How much does Etsy charge?

Etsy charges listing, transaction, and country-specific payment-processing fees. Some sellers also have a regulatory fee or an Offsite Ads fee when Etsy attributes a sale to an ad.

How do I calculate Etsy profit?

Add item price and buyer-paid shipping, then subtract modeled Etsy fees, production costs, packaging and shipping costs, and any allocated Etsy Plus subscription cost.

How do I calculate Etsy fees?

Estimate the listing fee, 6.5% transaction fee, seller-country payment-processing fee, any modeled regulatory operating fee, and any attributed Offsite Ads fee.

How do I calculate profit margin?

Divide net profit by gross revenue (item price plus buyer-paid shipping) and multiply by 100.

What is an Etsy break-even price?

It is the lowest item price that covers the modeled fees and costs with your shipping, seller country, and ad settings held constant.

How do Offsite Ads affect profit?

An attributed order may incur a 12% or 15% fee according to the selected calculator setting, subject to the modeled country fee cap.

What fees apply to Etsy digital products?

Listing, transaction, payment-processing, and attributed advertising fees can still apply. Set production and packaging to zero only if they do not apply to your product.

Does ShopProfit store my financial data?

No. Calculations happen in your browser. Inputs and results are not sent to ShopProfit servers; report sharing happens only when you choose it.
