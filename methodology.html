How ShopProfit estimates Etsy take-home

ShopProfit estimates one order at a time from the price, buyer-paid shipping, seller country, selected Offsite Ads rate, Etsy Plus setting, and the costs entered in the calculator.

Revenue and fees

gross revenue = item price + shipping charged to buyer

The estimate subtracts the listing fee, Etsy’s 6.5% transaction fee on gross revenue, country-specific payment processing, any modeled regulatory operating fee, and an applicable Offsite Ads fee. Offsite Ads are capped using the country data in the calculator.

Costs and net profit

net profit = gross revenue − platform fees − production cost − packaging/shipping cost − Etsy Plus allocation

The Etsy Plus allocation is the monthly subscription estimate divided by the sales-per-month setting. Cost inputs are treated as nonnegative amounts. Calculations use integer minor currency units and round fee components to the nearest minor unit.

Margin and prices

profit margin = net profit ÷ gross revenue × 100

Break-even and target-profit prices are found by searching for the lowest item price whose calculated net reaches the selected target while keeping the entered buyer shipping and other settings unchanged.

Scope and limitations

Fee information is based on Etsy's published fee schedules and may change. Check Etsy's current fee policy for the latest applicable fees. Fee schedule reviewed: October 2026. Fixed charges published in USD are shown as static local-currency estimates where needed; currency conversion is not modeled. Sales tax, VAT on seller fees, refunds, deposit fees, currency-conversion charges, and account-specific adjustments are not modeled. Payment processing can also include applicable sales tax, which this calculator does not accept as an input. Canada and Australia use the documented domestic processing rate by default; international rates differ. Global / Other is a generic USD baseline, not a country-specific schedule.
