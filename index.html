The short answer

How much does Etsy take from a sale?

Etsy charges a 6.5% transaction fee on the order total, plus a $0.20 USD listing fee and country-specific payment processing. Some sellers also pay a regulatory operating fee or an Offsite Ads fee of 12% or 15% when Etsy attributes the order to an ad.

Your exact total depends on seller location, order value, and ad attribution.

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about fees, shipping, and your data.

What fees does Etsy charge sellers?

Most sellers pay a listing fee, a 6.5% transaction fee, and payment processing based on the seller’s bank country. Certain countries have regulatory operating fees. Attributed Offsite Ads orders can add a 12% or 15% fee.

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

Yes. Etsy’s transaction fee applies to the order total including shipping charged to the buyer. Payment processing generally also applies to the sale amount including shipping.

How does Etsy Offsite Ads affect profit?

When Etsy attributes an order to an Offsite Ad, the fee is typically 15%, or 12% for shops that have reached Etsy’s sales threshold. Etsy also applies a per-order cap. The calculator shows the selected rate as an estimate.

How is payment processing calculated?

It is a percentage of the payment plus a fixed amount per order. Both values depend on the seller’s bank account country; international orders may use a different rate in some regions.

How do I calculate Etsy profit?

Net profit = item price + buyer shipping − Etsy fees − production and material costs − packaging and shipping costs. Include other business costs that apply to your sale.

How do I calculate Etsy fees?

Add the listing fee, transaction fee on the order total, payment processing for the seller’s country, any regulatory operating fee, and any Offsite Ads fee attributed to the order.

How much should I charge on Etsy?

Choose a price that covers your costs and Etsy fees while meeting your take-home target. Use break-even for the minimum price that avoids a loss, or target pricing for a chosen profit amount.

What is Etsy profit margin?

Profit margin is net profit divided by gross revenue, then multiplied by 100. ShopProfit’s gross revenue includes item price and shipping charged to the buyer.

What is an Etsy break-even price?

It is the minimum item price that covers estimated Etsy fees and costs at your chosen shipping amount, seller location, and Offsite Ads rate.

Can I calculate digital product profit?

Yes. Choose the Digital product preset to set production and packaging costs to zero, then enter your item price and seller location.

Does ShopProfit store my financial data?

No. Your inputs and calculations stay in this browser. ShopProfit does not send them to a server or require an account. Sharing or downloading a report happens only when you choose it.
