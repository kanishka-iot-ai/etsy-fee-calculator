How ShopProfit Estimates Etsy Take-Home & Net Profit

Calculation Philosophy: ShopProfit estimates single-order Etsy economics by combining your pricing inputs, direct fulfillment costs, official Etsy fee schedules, and seller country regulations. All calculations are executed locally in the browser using integer-cent arithmetic (avoiding floating-point inaccuracies), providing instantaneous updates with 100% data privacy.

1. The Revenue & Deductions Formula

Every calculation reconciles gross customer payment with platform fees and direct business costs to arrive at true net take-home profit:

gross revenue = item price + shipping charged to buyer total platform fees = listing fee + transaction fee + payment processing + regulatory operating fee + offsite ads + etsy plus allocation net profit = gross revenue − platform fees − production cost − packaging/shipping cost − Etsy Plus allocation profit margin = (net profit ÷ gross revenue) × 100

Fee information is based on Etsy's published fee schedules and may change. Check Etsy's current fee policy for the latest applicable fees. Fee schedule reviewed: October 2026.

2. Individual Fee Component Mathematics

A. Listing Fee

Billed at $0.20 USD per listing. All fixed-cost platform fees are published by Etsy in USD and converted to your payment account currency at current market rates when the fee is posted; our models approximate these currency conversions (£0.16 GBP, CA$0.27 CAD, A$0.28 AUD, 0,18 € EUR, ₹16.50 INR, ￥30 JPY, ₺7.00 TRY), subject to floating foreign exchange fluctuations.

B. Transaction Fee (6.5%)

Calculated as 6.5% of gross revenue:

transaction fee = Math.round(gross revenue in cents × 0.065)

C. Payment Processing Fee

Varies based on seller location. Calculated on gross revenue plus any applicable buyer taxes:

processing fee = Math.round(gross revenue in cents × country processing rate) + country fixed fee in cents

D. Regulatory Operating Fee

Where applicable across 9 regulated jurisdictions (UK: 0.48%, France: 1.14%, Italy: 0.80%, Spain: 0.88%, Canada: 0.50%, Turkey: 1.67%, India: 0.05%, Vietnam: 1.24%, Hungary: 1.97%):

regulatory fee = Math.round(gross revenue in cents × country regulatory rate)

E. Offsite Ads Fee (0%, 12%, or 15%)

When an order is attributed to an external ad, the selected fee rate is assessed and capped at $100.00 USD (or equivalent in local currency):

offsite fee = Math.min(Math.round(gross revenue in cents × offsite rate), country offsite cap in cents)

F. Etsy Plus Proration

When the Etsy Plus subscription toggle is enabled, the monthly $10 subscription is prorated across your estimated monthly sales volume:

etsy plus per sale = Math.round(monthly plus cost in cents ÷ sales per month)

3. Pricing Helpers: Break-Even & Target Profit

A. Break-Even Price Calculation

The break-even price is the exact minimum item selling price required so that net profit equals $0.00, keeping buyer shipping, production costs, shipping costs, and advertising rates constant. ShopProfit solves for break-even through an exact cent-by-cent ascending search that guarantees mathematical convergence even under nonlinear fee caps.

B. Target Profit Price Calculation

Target profit pricing calculates the necessary item price to yield your desired dollar take-home amount after covering all platform deductions and fulfillment costs.
