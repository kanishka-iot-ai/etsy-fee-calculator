How ShopProfit Estimates Etsy Take-Home & Net Profit

Calculation Philosophy: ShopProfit estimates single-order Etsy economics by combining your pricing inputs, direct fulfillment costs, official Etsy fee schedules, and registered seller country regulations. All calculations are executed locally in the browser using integer-cent arithmetic (avoiding floating-point inaccuracies), providing instantaneous updates with 100% data privacy.

1. The Core Revenue & Net Profit Model

Every ShopProfit calculation reconciles customer payment with direct transaction platform fees, order fulfillment expenses, and optional account overhead to compute true take-home net profit. Order-level deductions are strictly separated from shop-level subscription amortizations:

gross revenue = item price + shipping charged to buyer + gift wrapping platform fees = listing fee + transaction fee + payment processing fee + regulatory operating fee + offsite ads fee order costs = production cost + packaging/shipping paid + optional marketing shop overhead = Etsy Plus monthly amortization net profit = gross revenue − platform fees − order costs − shop overhead profit margin % = (net profit ÷ gross revenue) × 100

Fee information is based on Etsy's published fee schedules and may change. Check Etsy's current fee policy for the latest applicable fees. Fee schedule reviewed: October 2026.

2. The Four Distinct Etsy Fee Bases

A common error in amateur fee estimates is applying every percentage fee to the same order sum. In Etsy's actual ledger accounting, different fees assess four distinct financial bases:

Fee Component Statutory Calculation Base Inclusions & Exclusions Official Policy Citation
Transaction Fee (6.5%) Item Price + Shipping Charged + Gift Wrap Charged on total buyer payment for the item and shipping. Excludes sales tax or VAT collected directly by Etsy. Help Center Article 115014483627
Payment Processing Fee Item Price + Shipping Charged + Gift Wrap + Buyer Tax/VAT Charged on the gross settled card payment. Payment processors charge their percentage on total funds transferred, including marketplace sales tax. Help Center Article 115015628847
Regulatory Operating Fee Item Price + Shipping Charged + Gift Wrap Assessed in 9 regulated countries to offset Digital Services Taxes. Explicitly excludes sales tax/VAT collected by Etsy. Help Center Article 1500011073202
Currency Conversion (2.5%) Converted Payout Amount Assessed when the currency of your item listing differs from the payout currency of your payment account. Help Center Article 360000344668

3. Individual Fee Component Mathematics

A. Listing Fee ($0.20 USD equivalent)

Fixed at $0.20 USD per listing. All fixed platform fees are published by Etsy in USD and converted to your payment account currency at current market rates when the fee is posted. Our models reflect standard published equivalents (£0.16 GBP, CA$0.27 CAD, A$0.28 AUD, 0,18 € EUR, ₹16.50 INR, ¥30 JPY, ₺7.00 TRY), subject to exchange-rate adjustments by Etsy.

B. Transaction Fee (6.5%)

Assessed as a flat 6.5% on the transaction base (item price + buyer shipping + gift wrapping):

transaction fee = Math.round(transaction base in cents × 0.065)

C. Payment Processing Fee (Country Schedule)

Assessed on the gross payment processing base according to your registered seller country:

processing fee = Math.round(processing base in cents × country processing rate) + country fixed fee in cents

Our calculator models domestic default schedules by default, with international buyer rate variations documented for dual-tier jurisdictions such as Canada (3.0% + $0.25 CAD domestic vs. 4.0% + $0.25 CAD international) and Australia (3.0% + A$0.25 domestic vs. 4.0% + A$0.25 international).

D. Regulatory Operating Fee (9 Statutory Jurisdictions)

Assessed exclusively in nine statutory jurisdictions where local governments levy Digital Services Taxes on platform operators:

  • United Kingdom: 0.48%
  • France: 1.14%
  • Italy: 0.80%
  • Spain: 0.88%
  • Canada: 0.50%
  • Türkiye: 1.67%
  • India: 0.05%
  • Vietnam: 1.24%
  • Hungary: 1.97%
regulatory fee = Math.round(regulatory base in cents × country regulatory rate)

All other sovereign jurisdictions are officially unlisted by Etsy (modeled as unassessed/null in ShopProfit metadata, not synthetic zeroes).

E. Offsite Ads Fee (0%, 12%, or 15% with Statutory Cap)

When an order is attributed to an external advertising click within a 30-day window, Etsy charges an advertising fee based on trailing 12-month sales volume:

  • 15% optional tier: Applied to shops earning under $10,000 USD in trailing 365-day gross sales (sellers may opt out).
  • 12% mandatory tier: Applied to shops earning $10,000 USD or more in trailing 365-day gross sales (mandatory for the lifetime of the shop).
  • Statutory fee cap: Under official Etsy policy, the maximum Offsite Ads fee on any single attributed order is strictly $100.00 USD (or local currency equivalent converted at settlement).
offsite fee = Math.min(Math.round(gross revenue in cents × offsite rate), statutory cap in cents)

ShopProfit's fee engine represents the cap internally as { amount: 100, currency: "USD", type: "usd_equivalent" } without hardcoding approximate local fiat caps (£80, CA$135) or inventing synthetic exchange rates.

F. Currency Conversion Fee (2.5%)

When a seller lists products in a currency different from their bank account deposit currency, Etsy applies a 2.5% currency conversion charge on the payment amount. For single-currency domestic listings, this conversion fee is $0.00.

G. Etsy Plus Monthly Amortization

When the optional Etsy Plus subscription toggle is enabled, the fixed $10.00 USD/month subscription (or local currency equivalent) is amortized across your estimated monthly sales volume:

etsy plus per sale = Math.round(monthly plus cost in cents ÷ sales per month)

5. Pricing Solvers: Integer Binary Search Algorithm

A. Break-Even Price Solver

The unit break-even price is the exact minimum item selling price required for net profit to equal $0.00 while keeping shipping, production costs, and marketing selections constant. ShopProfit solves for break-even through an integer binary-search algorithm that guarantees convergence in under 32 iterations ($O(\log N)$) while accounting for component-level integer rounding and fee caps. Explore our standalone Etsy break-even calculator for targeted unit economics.

B. Target Profit Price Solver

Target profit pricing calculates the item price required to achieve an exact dollar net profit target after all platform deductions and expenses. The solver executes the same logarithmic binary search against the unified calculation engine. Plan custom pricing strategies with our Etsy pricing calculator.

9. Limitations & Practical Disclaimers

While ShopProfit strives for exact calculation accuracy, sellers should be aware of standard operational parameters:

  • Policy Changes: Etsy updates payment processing rates, regulatory surcharges, and country availability periodically. Always refer to your active shop dashboard for contractual updates.
  • Foreign Exchange: Fixed USD charges ($0.20 listing fee, $100 Offsite Ads cap) are converted into local settlement currencies by Etsy using daily bank exchange rates.
  • Taxes and Withholdings: Local sales taxes, VAT, GST, and income taxes vary by seller circumstances, tax residency, and registration thresholds.
  • Independent Service: ShopProfit is an independent educational and analytical utility. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Etsy, Inc.
  • Verification: Always verify your actual disbursements and ledger entries against your official Etsy Payment Account statement.
