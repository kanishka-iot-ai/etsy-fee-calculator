How Etsy Fees Work: 2026 Direct Answer

Direct Answer: In 2026, Etsy charges sellers three core platform fees on every completed sale: a $0.20 USD listing fee per item (renewed every 4 months or upon sale), a 6.5% transaction fee on the total order amount in the designated listing currency (item price + buyer shipping + gift wrap), and country-specific Etsy Payments processing fees (typically 3% to 6.5% + a fixed charge). In addition, sellers in 9 statutory countries pay a regulatory operating fee (0.05% to 1.97%), and sellers whose orders originate from Etsy's external marketing pay a 12% or 15% Offsite Ads fee (strictly capped at $100 USD per order).

1. The Core Etsy Seller Fees Explained

Whether you sell handmade ceramics, vintage clothing, craft supplies, or digital printables, Etsy deducts platform fees automatically from your payment account when an order takes place. Understanding the exact calculation basis for each fee is essential for setting profitable selling prices.

A. Etsy Listing Fee ($0.20 USD)

Etsy charges $0.20 USD to publish a listing for sale. Key rules governing listing fees include:

  • Duration: A listing remains active for 4 months or until the inventory sells out.
  • Auto-Renewal: Listings auto-renew after 4 months unless canceled, incurring another $0.20 fee.
  • Multi-Quantity Sales: If you list a quantity of 5 items, you pay $0.20 upfront for the first unit. When a buyer purchases 3 units in a single checkout, Etsy charges two $0.20 auto-renew sold fees for the 2 additional units purchased.
  • Currency: Listing fees are always billed as $0.20 USD. If your payment account currency is CAD, GBP, EUR, AUD, or INR, Etsy converts $0.20 USD into your local currency at their prevailing exchange rate.

B. Etsy Transaction Fee (6.5%)

When you make a sale on Etsy, you are charged a transaction fee of 6.5% of the total order amount in your designated listing currency. This fee applies to the total cost of the item, along with shipping and gift wrap (if you charge your customers for these):

transaction fee = 6.5% × (item price + shipping charged + gift wrap)

Etsy charges the transaction fee on shipping specifically to discourage sellers from listing an item for a low price and inflating shipping charges to evade platform fees.

Distinguishing the three statutory fee bases:

  • Transaction fee basis: 6.5% of the total order amount in your designated listing currency, applying to item price, shipping charged, and gift wrap.
  • Payment processing basis: Assessed on the total payment amount received from the buyer, which may include applicable sales tax or VAT collected by Etsy where applicable.
  • Regulatory operating fee basis: Assessed on item price plus shipping charged and gift wrap, and explicitly excludes any sales tax collected by Etsy.

C. Etsy Payments Processing Fee

Most transactions are processed through Etsy Payments. Unlike the transaction fee, the payment processing fee varies by the seller's registered bank account country and consists of a percentage plus a fixed flat fee:

  • United States: 3.0% + $0.25 USD
  • United Kingdom: 4.0% + £0.20 GBP
  • Canada: 3.0% + CA$0.25 (domestic or US orders); 4.0% + CA$0.25 (international orders)
  • Australia: 3.0% + A$0.25 (domestic orders); 4.0% + A$0.25 (international orders)
  • Germany & Eurozone: 4.0% + 0,30 €
  • France, Italy & Spain: 4.0% + 0,30 € (plus respective regulatory operating fees)
  • India: 5.0% + ₹25.00 INR
  • Japan: 6.0% + ￥45 JPY (equivalent fixed charge)
  • Türkiye: 6.5% + ₺14.00 TRY

Basis note: Payment processing is assessed on the entire gross transaction amount collected, which includes item price, shipping charged, and any buyer sales tax or VAT collected by Etsy where applicable.

D. Regulatory Operating Fees (9 Statutory Jurisdictions)

To offset Digital Services Taxes (DST) imposed by individual national governments on digital platforms, Etsy passes along a regulatory operating fee in specific countries. This fee is calculated as a percentage of the item price plus buyer shipping and gift wrap (if applicable), and explicitly excludes any sales tax collected by Etsy:

  • United Kingdom: 0.48%
  • France: 1.14%
  • Hungary: 1.97%
  • India: 0.05%
  • Italy: 0.80%
  • Spain: 0.88%
  • Türkiye: 1.67%
  • Canada: 0.50%
  • Vietnam: 1.24%

Statutory coverage note: Jurisdictions not listed by Etsy (such as the United States, Australia, and Germany) are not assessed for a regulatory operating fee. In our fee intelligence engine and documentation, these jurisdictions are explicitly recorded as not listed by Etsy, rather than carrying an explicit 0% rate. E. Etsy Offsite Ads (12% or 15% with Statutory Cap)

Etsy markets products across external advertising channels including Google Shopping, Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest, and Bing. You do not pay for impressions or clicks—you only pay an advertising fee if a buyer clicks an external ad and purchases from your shop within a 30-day attribution window:

  • 15% Fee (Under $10,000 Threshold): 15% if the shop has always remained below $10,000 USD in any consecutive 365-day period. Offsite Ads is optional for these shops, and you may choose to opt out in your shop settings.
  • 12% Fee (At or Above $10,000 Threshold): 12% once the shop has reached $10,000 USD or more in a consecutive 365-day period. Once this milestone is reached, participation is mandatory for the lifetime of the shop even if sales subsequently drop.
  • 30-Day Attribution Window: An Offsite Ads fee is attributed when a buyer clicks an Offsite Ad and purchases from your shop within 30 days of that click.
  • $100 USD Fee Cap: Regardless of the order size, Etsy strictly caps the maximum Offsite Ads fee at $100.00 USD (or local currency equivalent converted by Etsy at settlement) per attributed order.

F. Shipping Fee Economics: Why Fees Apply to Shipping

A frequent surprise for new sellers is discovering that Etsy fees apply to postage. Etsy charges both the 6.5% transaction fee and payment processing on the postage amount paid by the buyer. If you charge $10 shipping, Etsy takes roughly $1.00 in platform fees directly from that shipping charge. If your actual carrier postage costs $10, you lose money on shipping unless your item price absorbs that shortfall.

2. Other Etsy Charges (Account-Level & Non-Transaction Costs)

Not all Etsy charges are deducted per sale. Several operational costs represent separate business expenses or account-level disbursements that are not automatically charged on every transaction:

  • Etsy Plus ($10/month): An optional monthly shop subscription offering 15 listing credits, $5 in Etsy Ads credits, custom banners, and restock notifications. In ShopProfit's calculator, this subscription can be prorated across your estimated monthly sales volume.
  • Etsy Ads (On-Platform CPC): Keyword and search marketing within Etsy's marketplace. Billed on a cost-per-click basis against a seller-defined daily budget, independent of order transaction fees.
  • Shipping Labels: Postage purchased directly through Etsy Shipping Labels (e.g., USPS, Canada Post, Royal Mail, Australia Post, FedEx). These are billed as direct postage costs to your payment account.
  • Statutory Deposit & Bank Transfer Fees: In 9 countries (Indonesia, Israel, Malaysia, Mexico, Morocco, Philippines, South Africa, Türkiye, Vietnam), Etsy assesses a fixed bank transfer fee when scheduled disbursements fall below statutory thresholds (under official Help Center Article 115015628847 Table 2). These apply per bank payout event, not per individual sale.
  • Currency Conversion (2.5%): If you list items in a currency different from your registered bank payout currency, Etsy assesses a 2.5% currency conversion surcharge on the converted settlement funds.

3. Why Etsy Fees Vary by Country

Etsy seller fees are not identical worldwide. Platform deductions vary based on five primary regulatory and operational factors:

  1. Local Banking Infrastructure: Payment processing percentages and fixed transaction charges reflect local interchange agreements and banking partner costs in each sovereign market.
  2. National Digital Taxes: Governments in the UK, France, Italy, Spain, Canada, Türkiye, India, Vietnam, and Hungary levy Digital Services Taxes, which Etsy offsets via country-specific regulatory operating fees.
  3. Payout Disbursement Schedules: Certain international markets require minimum deposit thresholds or fixed administrative bank transfer fees on smaller disbursements.
  4. Floating Foreign Exchange Rates: Etsy sets fixed baseline fees in USD ($0.20 listing fee, $100 Offsite Ads cap), which fluctuate when converted into local fiat currencies.
  5. Cross-Currency Listing Choices: Sellers listing in currencies outside their local bank deposit currency incur an additional 2.5% conversion fee.

6. Digital Products Fee Considerations

Digital downloads on Etsy have distinct economics. While physical items incur manufacturing materials, packaging boxes, and postal postage, digital items incur virtually zero variable delivery costs. Key aspects include:

  • Identical Etsy Fees: Digital listings incur the same $0.20 listing fee, 6.5% transaction fee, and payment processing fees as physical items.
  • Zero Shipping Deductions: Because digital files are delivered automatically through Etsy's download servers, buyer shipping is $0.00.
  • Higher Margins: Digital sellers routinely achieve 80% to 88% net margins after platform fees, making digital items highly resilient to fee increases.

8. Frequently Asked Questions About Etsy Fees

How much does Etsy charge sellers per sale?

Etsy charges a $0.20 USD listing fee, a 6.5% transaction fee on the total order amount in the designated listing currency (item price + shipping + gift wrap), and country-specific Etsy Payments processing fees (typically 3% to 6.5% plus a small fixed charge). Sellers in 9 countries also pay a regulatory operating fee (0.05% to 1.97%), and attributed Offsite Ads add 12% or 15%.

Does Etsy take a transaction fee on shipping?

Yes. Etsy's 6.5% transaction fee applies to the entire order total charged to the buyer, which includes the item price, shipping fees, and any gift wrap charges.

What is Etsy's payment processing fee?

Payment processing is charged on the total payment received from the buyer (including taxes if collected by Etsy). In the United States, it is 3% + $0.25 USD; in the UK, 4% + £0.20; in Canada and Australia, 3% + $0.25 for domestic sales; and in Europe, 4% + €0.30.

What is Etsy's regulatory operating fee?

A regulatory operating fee is a surcharge (between 0.05% and 1.97%) charged to sellers in countries with local digital services tax legislation, including the UK (0.48%), France (1.14%), Italy (0.80%), Spain (0.88%), Canada (0.50%), Türkiye (1.67%), Vietnam (1.24%), and Hungary (1.97%). Jurisdictions not listed by Etsy are not assessed for this fee, rather than carrying a statutory 0% rate.

How much do Etsy Offsite Ads cost?

Offsite Ads cost 15% if the shop has always remained below $10,000 USD in any consecutive 365-day period (optional), or 12% once the shop has reached $10,000 USD or more in a consecutive 365-day period (mandatory for the lifetime of the shop). Attributed when a buyer clicks an Offsite Ad and purchases within 30 days, strictly capped at $100 USD per attributed order.

What are Etsy fees for digital downloads?

Digital products pay the same $0.20 listing fee, 6.5% transaction fee, and payment processing fees as physical goods. Because digital files have $0 shipping and packaging costs, net profit margins are typically higher (80% to 88%).
